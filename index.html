Why we exist

Technology is for every child.

KidsInAITech is dedicated to helping children discover essential AI and coding skills in a fun, interactive environment. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide and inspire the next generation of innovators.

  • Make complex technology feel fun and approachable
  • Encourage creativity, collaboration, and confidence
  • Help more families connect with opportunities to learn

Who is KidsInAITech for?

KidsInAITech focuses on children ages 6–12 who are interested in discovering coding, AI, and technology in a welcoming environment.

Do kids need coding experience?

Our proposed activities are designed to welcome beginners and encourage exploration, regardless of prior experience.

Are registrations open?

This is a student project prototype. Program dates, availability, and actual enrollment have not been confirmed. The interest form demonstrates a proposed registration workflow.

Can I volunteer or donate?

The proposed site includes ways for community members to get involved. The current prototype does not collect real applications or accept payments.
