Every child deserves a chance to create the future.

We believe technology education should be welcoming, joyful, and within reach. KidsInAITech is all about making that belief a reality.

Our story & purpose

Bridging the digital divide.

KidsInAITech is a nonprofit organization dedicated to teaching children ages 6–12 essential AI technology and coding skills through interactive workshops, summer camps, and after-school programs.

Its mission is to encourage a love of technology early in life and empower the next generation of innovators, regardless of their background or access to technology.

Our north star

Big potential belongs to everyone.

We want learning about AI and coding to feel less like a barrier and more like an open invitation to explore, create, and dream bigger.

“Imagine it. Build it. Believe in it.” ✨
